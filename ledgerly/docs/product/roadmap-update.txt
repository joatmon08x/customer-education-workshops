What is planned
Next quarter adds mocked reminder calls for upcoming invoices and overdue invoices. The plan covers scheduling and quiet hours, scripts and tone, consent and opt-out, outcomes and transcripts on the invoice, retry rules, a call results view for the Finance lead, and a pay link after the call.

Order and why
Call scheduling and quiet hours comes first. Later work needs the lead times and the quiet hours.
Call scripts and tone, and Consent and opt-out, come next. They can overlap scheduling. Call outcomes and transcripts needs the default reminder script and consent recorded before the first call.
Call outcomes and transcripts comes before Retry rules and before Call results dashboard. Those two are blocked until outcomes finishes.
Pay link after the call is blocked by Call scripts and tone, because it needs the pay prompt in the script.
Retry rules is also blocked by Call routing.

Risks and open decisions
Three decisions are still unset. Which zone applies when the customer record has no local zone. How many days a <pay link> stays valid. How many days pass between a missed call and the retry.
Grok Voice stays a mock, so the quarter does not prove a live call.
Retry rules and Call results dashboard both sit in March, so review time in that month is tight.

Out of scope
No live call, no phone number, and no provider key.
The call does not change the amount due.
The mock does not store audio.
The app's Collections and Disputes areas are out of scope.
